# BRD — Smart Inbox Triage

## Business problem
The client's inbox is unmanageable in the mornings: too many emails, most requiring no attention. Time is lost sorting before real work begins.

## Objective
Give the client a **clear morning summary**: only the emails that need their action, with the next step for each — reducing noise to zero touches.

## Success criteria
- The client can see, in under 30 seconds, what needs action today.
- Newsletters / automated mail never appear in the action list.
- Every action item carries a concrete next step, not just a subject line.

## Scope
**In scope (v1):** rule-based triage of an email list into Needs Action / FYI / Noise; morning summary card; next-step suggestion per email; dark mode; sample inbox for demo.
**Out of scope (v1):** live Gmail connection, sending replies, mobile app.

## Stakeholders
- Client (inbox owner) — primary user.
- Builder (course learner) — owner of the tool.
